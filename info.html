About me

I’ve always been fascinated by patterns—in how images fit together, how stories unfold, and how seemingly unrelated details connect.

Whether I’m editing a video or reporting a story, finding those connections helps me make sense of what I’m seeing and share it with an audience.

( Snuck in some pictures of my parents visiting me in San Francisco! )

My background in film production

In 2014, I came to the United States with a dream of working in Hollywood. Before that, I was a couch potato watching far too much Die Hard and Transformers on the 32-inch TV in my parents’ home. Watching explosions and futuristic worlds unfold on screen, I wanted to learn how to create those visuals myself.

I started learning After Effects, making fake car crashes and staging gunfights with images of bullet holes. Then I discovered Premiere Pro, and editing became a bigger part of my life.

A storytelling bottleneck

By 2023, while I was in college, I knew how to use the tools, but I was struggling to find something worth saying. The fictional stories I came up with felt like familiar Hollywood dramas. The scenes I thought would look cool had already appeared in Die Hard decades earlier.

That made me reconsider what I wanted to do with those skills. Maybe I could use them to tell real stories—stories about people’s lives, with consequences beyond the screen.

Finding journalism

Journalism felt like a new purpose for the skills I already had. My background in film production gave me different ways to approach a story: through video, sound, images, and text. Reporting gave me questions I wanted to pursue and a reason to choose the medium that would help people understand the answers.

What draws me to this work is the chance to connect the details, explain something complicated, and help information reach the people who need it.

My mission

I want to live in a world where people understand one another more clearly. Through reporting and visual storytelling, I hope to close some of the gaps between what people mean, what others hear, and what they need to know.
